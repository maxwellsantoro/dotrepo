A `Docs:`, `Documentation:`, `Getting started:` or `Quickstart:` prefix must form a complete declaration, assessed with its paragraph subject and qualifiers. A physical prefix cannot override preceding integration prose or a trailing “for Other” qualifier. A paragraph made entirely of complete direct/nav declaration rows may retain those independent declarations, including adjacent documentation/start rows. This property is computed once in the paragraph cache; per-link checks use that cached result and the current rendered line. Multi-link prose short-circuits whole-paragraph prefix checks. One list marker and emphasis around the prefix token are supported. Neutral titles, wrapped complete declarations and explicit current-project subjects remain eligible; differently named subject labels remain subject to the original compatibility check.
