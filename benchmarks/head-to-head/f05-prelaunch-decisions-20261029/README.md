# F-05 prelaunch decisions — October 29

This addendum records coordinator decisions after the [remaining-eight source
proposal](../f05-qualification-next8-20261029/README.md), its original-curator
follow-up and a separate source challenge. It preserves those frozen assessments,
including their disagreements. It does not change the twelve enrolled tasks,
their revisions, the frozen public responses or the experiment's causal design.

Representation, source establishment and consumer benefit remain separate gates.
The internal runtime now preserves bounded, inventoried source links; its
[independent review](../f05-qualification-next8-20261029/source-links-review/README.md)
found no actionable defect within that scope. This qualifies source admission and
owned fixture provisioning, not upstream task execution or an agent environment.
Dependency caches remain regular files only. The original Go oracle is retained;
future Go qualifications use the append-only revision that rejects nested skips.

For MiroFish, a filename or print-only successful process does not establish a
mandatory semantic test or prove adequate coverage. For youtube-dl, source-disabled
SOCKS stand-ins cannot establish real SOCKS behavior. Unexpected skips, hidden
network/proxy early returns, setup or collection failures, interruption and missing
scope remain noncompletion or unresolved. A complete native assertion failure may
complete the task of obtaining the test outcome while leaving the suite failing;
report those dimensions separately. Do not introduce exclusions after seeing a
run's result.

These are prelaunch policy decisions, not executable oracle qualification. The
task-specific accepted native sets, exclusions, observation rules and synthetic
counterexamples still need a later content-bound freeze. No paid sessions are
authorized by this packet. Provider/model, inference settings, complete image and
dependency qualifications, numeric budgets, allocation, assignment and scorer
bindings remain launch prerequisites under the [owning specification](../experiments/applicability-2026-10-29/f05.md).

E1 development and qualification remain independent of this campaign. The exposed
E1 cases are known development workloads; a later generalization claim requires
fresh holdouts. F-05 keeps tasks requiring fallback and analyzes every assignment,
including lookup nonuse and failures. A current negative result remains useful
and must not be replaced by a later improved result.

`decision.json` binds the reviewed inputs and this bounded disposition. Its
integrity manifest does not authenticate external execution. Preparation failures,
unknown costs and unqualified tasks remain visible in their owning packets.
